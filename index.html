What I Solve

The kinds of problems I naturally gravitate towards

The work I enjoy most usually sits in ambiguity, when things are not fully formed yet, priorities are competing, or people know something needs to happen but are not fully clear on what that should look like.

✦

I make sense of messy things

When ideas, data, opinions, and business goals are all tangled together, I help find the thread. I like bringing shape to things that feel scattered, unclear, or stuck.

↗

I bridge strategy and reality

I don’t just like big ideas. I like understanding how they land, what it takes to execute them, and what needs to happen next for them to become real.

◎

I’m useful in the room and behind the scenes

I can facilitate, ask sharp questions, build the narrative, manage the moving parts, and quietly keep things going. I care about both the thinking and the follow-through.

In most projects, I naturally end up helping people get clearer, move faster, or make better decisions.

Working across strategy, project management, and delivery

Across projects, I’ve often played multiple roles at once: helping shape the thinking, managing stakeholders, keeping timelines and moving parts on track, and making sure the work can actually happen. How I Think

A few principles I keep coming back to

These are some of the patterns that shape how I approach work, people, and problem-solving.

?

Ask the question behind the question

A client may ask for a deck, a campaign, or a workshop, but the real need is usually alignment, clarity, confidence, momentum, or better thinking. I like getting underneath the ask, not just responding to it at face value.

✓

Make things useful, not just impressive

Good work should not only look polished. It should help people decide, move, align, or see something differently. I care about work that does something, not just work that looks good in a room.

∞

Bridge logic and creativity

I’m most comfortable where structure meets imagination, using both to solve problems in a way that feels grounded but fresh. I like work that is thoughtful, practical, and a little sharper than expected.

At my best, I help bring shape, direction, and momentum to things that are still forming.

Who I Am

A little about me

I’m someone who enjoys making sense of messy things. I think best when there is a knot to untangle, a vague idea to sharpen, or a room full of people who need help getting clearer on what matters.

I’ve always been drawn to the space between thinking and doing, where ideas need structure, communication, buy-in, and practical follow-through to actually go somewhere. A lot of the roles and projects I’ve taken on may look different on paper, but the thread has stayed the same: I like helping things make sense, helping people move forward, and making work more useful than it was before.
